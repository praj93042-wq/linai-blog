Enterprise adoption of AI agents accelerated significantly in early 2026, with 35% of large enterprises deploying autonomous agents in at least one business function.

The shift from pilot projects to production deployments marks a structural inflection point, driven by LLM-native architectures replacing traditional RPA.

The enterprise AI agent market is entering a maturity phase where infrastructure reliability and governance frameworks will determine which deployments scale and which stall.
